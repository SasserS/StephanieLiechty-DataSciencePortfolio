Employee Engagement and Productivity: The Hidden Cost

This folder contains resources, analyses, and findings regarding the significant impact of employee engagement on productivity and company financial performance. The documents inside illustrate how disengaged employees are costing companies in terms of lost productivity, wasted resources, and lower profitability.

Key Insights:

31% of employees are actively engaged: Data and research show that only a third of the workforce is truly engaged in their work, leading to a significant loss in potential productivity (Gallup, What Is Employee Engagement and How Do You Improve It?).

Financial Consequences: Companies with low employee engagement levels face higher turnover rates, decreased performance, and inefficiencies, which can result in millions of dollars in wasted costs annually.

Improving Engagement: The files in this folder also highlight potential solutions for improving employee engagement and boosting overall productivity, including best practices for leadership, communication, and organizational culture.

Purpose:

The aim of this folder is to provide evidence and insights into the growing issue of employee disengagement and its financial toll on businesses. By understanding these dynamics, companies can implement strategies that increase engagement and, in turn, improve both employee satisfaction and the company’s bottom line.

Feel free to review the data and analysis, and consider how these insights can apply to your organization or area of interest.
